How it works

From a one-hour video to a week of shorts.

1

Paste a link or upload

Any YouTube video, podcast, interview or stream. Pick a reel length: 10, 20, 30, 60 or 90 seconds.

2

AI finds the best moments

The full transcript is read and every strong moment is scored: hot takes, tips, stories, emotional and funny parts. Each becomes its own reel.

3

Edit, then upload

Change the headline, fix a caption, cut out a bad part, then send it to YouTube with your own title and description.

Features

Everything a short needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Finds the moments

AI reads the whole video, not just the loud parts, and picks moments that work on their own for a stranger.

English captions, always

Hindi, Urdu or any language goes in; clean English captions, headline and title come out.

Headline layout

Big text on top, the video in the middle, captions and a line of text below. Inside every platform's safe area.

Cut the bad parts

Mark a part to remove, cut a whole caption line, or drop every long pause in one click. Captions follow the cut.

Upload to YouTube

Sign in with YouTube once. Then upload any short with its title, description, tags and visibility, right from the editor.

Built for volume

A fair queue, background rendering and per-clip re-renders, so a whole team can clip at once.

Caption styles

Twelve animated caption styles.

Word-by-word timing on every one. Switch styles per clip in the editor.
